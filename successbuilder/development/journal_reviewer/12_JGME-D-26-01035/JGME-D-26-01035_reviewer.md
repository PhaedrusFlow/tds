   3 | Introduction
   4 | Educational Milestones were implemented for the assessment of graduate medical trainees by the
   5 | Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME) in 2013.1,2 One goal of these
   6 | milestones was to continue the shift in trainee assessment toward a competency-based medical
   7 | education (CBME) framework.3,4 The Milestones assess trainees along an expected developmental
   8 | trajectory across the six core competencies: interpersonal and communication skills, medical
   9 | knowledge, patient care, practice-based learning and improvement, professionalism, and systems-
  10 | based practice.

  11 | After the initial Milestones were developed, the ACGME underwent an iterative review process,
  12 | creating a revised set of milestones termed “Milestones 2.0.”2 Milestone data are reported to the
  13 | ACGME nationally for accreditation, but little is known about how programs use these data to
  14 | operationalize CBME in practice. The core components of CBME5 provide a framework for
  15 | examining how Milestones inform: outcome competencies, sequenced progression, tailored
  16 | learning experiences, competency-focused coaching, and programmatic assessment. This study
  17 | analyzed the results from a national survey of stakeholders in Graduate Medical Education (GME)
  18 | to understand how the Milestones 2.0 framework is being used to promote the core components of
  19 | CBME.
