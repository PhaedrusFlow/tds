1. Minimum copper EGC for 15‑A OCPD

Correct choice: 14 AWG

Code reference:

Table 250.122 – Minimum Size Equipment Grounding Conductors
The row for “Rating or setting of automatic overcurrent device in circuit not exceeding 20 amperes” lists 14 AWG copper for 15‑ampere devices.

2. GEC size for 2/0 Cu service conductors
Correct choice: 4 AWG

Code reference:

250.66 – Size of Alternating‑Current Grounding Electrode Conductor
The table in 250.66 shows that for largest ungrounded copper service conductors 2/0 AWG through 3/0 AWG, the minimum GEC is 4 AWG copper.

3. Working space depth, ≤1000 V, Condition 2
Correct choice: 4 ft (1.2 m)

Code reference:

110.26(A)(1) – Depth of Working Space

Table 110.26(A)(1) (or equivalent table in your edition)
For equipment 0–150 V to ground, Condition 2 requires 1.0 m (3.5 ft) in some editions; for 151–600 V to ground, Condition 2 requires 1.2 m (4 ft). Use the row and voltage category matching the exam’s assumption; many exam questions key to 4 ft for Condition 2 at 120/240 V. Check your specific table and match the row your prep material is using.

(If your table shows 3 ft for Condition 1 and 3.5 ft for Condition 2 at 120/240 V, your exam provider may frame the voltage or condition slightly differently—tab the table and verify before test day.)

4. Minimum working‑space height (≤1000 V)
Correct choice: 6.5 ft (2.0 m)

Code reference:

110.26(A)(3) – Height of Working Space
This section requires the working space to be not less than 2.0 m (6½ ft) high or the height of the equipment, whichever is greater.

5. Burial depth for RMC under residential driveway (passenger cars)
Correct choice: 18 inches

Code reference:

300.5 – Underground Installations

Table 300.5 – Minimum Cover Requirements
Look for the row “Rigid metal conduit” under “Residential driveways and outdoor parking areas, used only for dwelling‑related parking and subject to only passenger vehicle traffic.” The table gives 18 in. of cover.

6. Box‑fill volume per 12 AWG conductor
Correct choice: 2.25 in³

Code reference:

314.16(B) – Number of Conductors in Outlet, Device, and Junction Boxes

The table under 314.16(B) lists volume allowances. For 12 AWG, the required volume per conductor is 2.25 cubic inches.

7. Minimum clear height of working space (over 1000 V equipment)
Correct choice: 7 ft (2.1 m)

Code reference:

110.34(C) – Height of Working Space (for over 1000 V)
This requires working‑space height not less than 2.1 m (7 ft), or the height of the equipment, whichever is greater.

8. Service‑drop clearance over public streets/parking areas subject to truck traffic
Correct choice: 18 ft

Code reference:

230.24(B) – Clearance over Roads, Driveways, Walks, Yards, and Other Locations
The row for “public streets, alleys, roads, and parking areas subject to truck traffic” requires not less than 5.5 m (18 ft) clearance for conductors not exceeding 1000 volts.

9. Maximum spacing so no point along wall is more than X from a receptacle (dwelling)
Correct choice: 6 ft

Code reference:

210.52(A)(1) – Spacing
This subsection states that receptacle outlets must be installed so that no point measured horizontally along the floor line in any wall space is more than 1.8 m (6 ft) from a receptacle outlet. The “6‑ft rule” is what you’re testing here.

10. Demand load for a single 10‑kW range at 120/240 V
Correct choice: 8 kW

Code reference:

220.55 – Electric Ranges and Other Cooking Appliances

Table 220.55 – Demand Factors and Loads for Household Electric Ranges
For one range with a nameplate rating between 3½ and 12 kW, Note 1 allows using the nameplate rating from Column C, which is 8 kW for a single range in that rating band. (Some editions show this directly in the table; tab both 220.55 and its table.)
